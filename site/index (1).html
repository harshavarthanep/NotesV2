The writing surface

Hands stay on
the keyboard.

Every formatting move has a keystroke. The menus come to you, arrow keys pick, Enter inserts, and autosave never asks.

01 · SLASH COMMANDS

Type / and the whole editor opens up

Eleven insertions from one key. No menu hunting, no mouse.

  • Headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code — plus today's date and the current time.
  • [[ picks or creates a linked note; # autocompletes existing tags.
  • Paste a URL and it becomes a clickable link on its own.
  • Hover any block for a ▲▼ handle — move paragraphs without cutting and pasting.

02 · TIME MACHINE

Every draft you didn't mean to lose

ZenDocs snapshots your note locally as you write. Drag the slider, see exactly which lines differ, restore — and undo the restore if you change your mind.

  • Changed lines are highlighted against the current version.
  • Stored on this device only — no version history in the cloud.

Drag the slider in the panel — it's real.

03 · GRAPH VIEW Ctrl G

Bigger dot, more minds pointing at it

Node size is how many notes link in. Colour is the folder. Hover to spotlight a note and everything unrelated dims away.

  • Drag a dot or its label to arrange; drag empty space to pan.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom; auto-fits on open.
  • Tap a note to open it, tap a #tag to filter by it.
  • Unlinked mentions surface notes that name this one without linking — discovery at zero extra database reads.

Hover a node in the panel to try the spotlight.

04 · LOCAL AI & STUDY MODE

AI that never leaves the device

Summaries and suggested tags run on your machine — instant, private, no model download, no API key, no upload. Study mode turns your headings and bold terms into flashcards.

  • Insert the summary at the top of the note, or copy it out.
  • Guided mode walks the note section by section.

Tap the flashcard to flip it.

05 · CANVAS

Think in space, not in lists

Double-click empty space for an idea card. Connect two cards. Group them in a frame and the whole frame drags together.

  • Every card is a real note — colour it, duplicate it, open it.
  • Resize frames from the corner; double-tap the label to rename.
  • Saves only when something actually changed.

06 · BOARD, CALENDAR & MATRIX

Three more ways to look at the same work

Drag cards between To do, In progress and Done — or tap ◀ ▶ on a phone. The calendar marks every day you wrote with ☀ and counts what you created. The Eisenhower matrix shows what you've been avoiding.

  • Home aggregates pinned notes, every open task across every note, today's daily note and a 7-day completion chart.
  • Tap the month name for a 1950–2100 picker.
  • Templates — Meeting, Project, Cornell, Weekly review, plus your own with {{date}} {{title}} {{clipboard}} filled in on use.

Install ZenDocs

Under 1 MB · full screen · works offline

1Tap Open ZenDocs below — it opens in Safari. iOS only allows installs from Safari.
2Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
3Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
4The ZenDocs icon lands on your home screen and opens full screen — no Safari bars.

Apple doesn't let a website install itself on iOS; the Share-sheet tap is required by Safari. ZenDocs will also prompt you itself once open.

1Tap Open ZenDocs below in Chrome.
2ZenDocs shows its own install prompt — tap Get the app.
3Dismissed it? Menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.

Installs as a real launcher entry with its own app-switcher card.

1Open ZenDocs in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
2Click the install icon in the address bar — a small screen with a down arrow.
3Choose Install. ZenDocs gets its own window, taskbar and Dock icon.

Safari on Mac: File → Add to Dock.
