Install as an app PWA

ZenDocs installs from the browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. Same icon, same full-screen app, one tap from your home screen.

Takes essentially no storage. There's no 200 MB download — just an icon and a small offline shell, under 1 MB. Your notes live in the cloud and in the browser's cache.
Full screen, no browser bars. Launches like a native app with its own icon and splash.
Opens offline. The shell is cached, so it starts on a plane and syncs when you land.
Updates silently. No store queue, no update button — the newest version is whatever you open.

Add ZenDocs to your home screen

Under 1 MB · full screen · works offline

1Tap Open ZenDocs below. It opens in Safari — this only works in Safari on iOS.
2Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
3Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
4The ZenDocs icon appears on your home screen and opens full screen, no Safari bars.

Apple doesn't allow websites to install themselves on iOS — the Share sheet tap is required by Safari. ZenDocs will remind you with its own prompt when you open it.

1Tap Open ZenDocs below in Chrome.
2ZenDocs shows its own Install prompt. Tap Get the app.
3If you dismissed it: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.

Installs as a real app entry in your launcher and app switcher.

1Open ZenDocs in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
2Click the install icon in the address bar — a small screen with a down arrow.
3Choose Install. ZenDocs gets its own window, taskbar icon and Dock entry.

On Safari for Mac: File → Add to Dock.
